What does the processing fee cover?

The fee IS the price — $4.95 Standard, $8.95 Custom, plus delivery chosen at checkout. No additional fees, ever. The Council forbids drip pricing.

What is drip pricing? (And why does the Council forbid it?)

Drip pricing is when a seller advertises one price, then drips extra fees on you at checkout — service fees, processing fees, facility charges. Concert tickets are the classic offender. The Council considers this dishonorable conduct. Every price on this page IS the fee.

Who gets the kit — me or them?

Them. You pay the fee and enter the recipient's name and mailing address. The kit is mailed to your nominee.
